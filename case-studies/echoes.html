Echoes — Dream journal

A mobile dream journal app that helps users capture, reflect on, and find patterns in their dreams.

UX / UI Mobile app Immersive glass interface

About the project

What the product is and what I was solving

Context

I had to design a mobile dream journal app that helps users capture, reflect on, and find patterns in their dreams. It was heavily inspired by Carl Jung.

Problem

  • — Solve the problem of quickly and easily logging a dream right after waking up;
  • — Solve the problem of losing track of recurring dreams and symbols;
  • — Help users notice emotional and symbolic patterns across their dreams;
  • — Expand engagement through a gentle, atmospheric user experience;
  • — Design based on human-centered design principles.
